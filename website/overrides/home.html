Measure how attacks and defenses change the way an autonomous vehicle drives.

AVSecTester is an open-source testbed for autonomous-vehicle security. Run a driving system in closed loop, in CARLA or in real-world drives reconstructed with NVIDIA NuRec, then attack it, add defenses and compare what the vehicle does.
